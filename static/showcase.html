4-Layer Multi-Defense Architecture WORD-INDEPENDENT DEFENSE

Traditional security models fail because attackers swap keywords. Voice Clone Shield solves this by fusing acoustic biology, dense latent embeddings, zero-shot intent reasoning, and conversational state tracking.

Layer 1 • Signal Physics
Acoustic Deepfake Forensics
Operates directly on the 16kHz raw waveform. Evaluates $F_0$ pitch jitter, shimmer, spectral flatness (synthesizer noise vs tone ratio), and high-frequency roll-off left by neural vocoders (HiFi-GAN, ElevenLabs).

Layer 2 • Representation
Dense Semantic Vector Embeddings
Uses all-MiniLM-L6-v2 to convert spoken sentences into 384-dimensional vectors. Computes cosine similarity against pre-computed fraud intent centroids (Emergency Bail, Tax Extortion, Kidnapping).

Layer 3 • Reasoning
Zero-Shot NLI Intent Reasoning
